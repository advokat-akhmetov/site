Understanding Economic Criminal Cases

When a Commercial Dispute Becomes a Criminal Investigation

Economic criminal cases often arise in connection with contractual relationships, corporate disagreements, financial transactions, performance of obligations and the management of business assets.

The existence of debt, non-performance of a contract, an unprofitable transaction or a dispute between business partners does not automatically mean that a criminal offense has occurred.

At the same time, describing a transaction as a commercial arrangement does not exclude criminal liability where the elements of an offense established by law are proven.

The advocate's task is to distinguish proven facts from assumptions, examine the legal nature of the parties' relationship and assess whether the criminal allegations are legally supported.

Key Questions to Examine

  • What obligations existed between the parties?
  • What actions were actually performed?
  • Who had authority and access to the assets?
  • Which documents support the transactions?
  • How was the alleged loss calculated?
  • What evidence supports the required mental elements of the offense?
  • Were criminal procedure requirements followed?

Misappropriation or Embezzlement — Article 189

Cases concerning property entrusted to particular individuals. Review of authority, asset movements, supporting records and the calculation of alleged losses.

Fraud and Commercial Transactions

Criminal Fraud or Breach of Contract: Understanding the Legal Difference

Civil and Commercial Disputes

The parties may have a valid contract involving payment, delivery of goods, performance of services or repayment of a loan.

Failure to perform contractual obligations may result from financial difficulties, a counterparty's breach, changes in market conditions or other ordinary business risks.

Criminal-Law Assessment

A fraud allegation requires proof of the objective and subjective elements of the relevant offense under Kazakhstan law.

Relevant evidence may include the content of negotiations, the circumstances in which property was obtained, transaction documents, subsequent conduct and other material facts.

Important: The existence of a contract does not automatically rule out fraud. Equally, a contractual breach does not by itself prove fraud. Each case requires an individual assessment of the evidence.

Investigative Actions

Searches, Document Seizures, Questioning and Asset Restrictions

Economic criminal investigations may involve investigative actions affecting accounting records, electronic devices, business premises and corporate assets.

Assessing the legality of these measures requires examining their procedural basis, the authority of officials, relevant documents and the circumstances in which the measures were carried out.

Depending on the client's procedural status, the advocate may participate in actions permitted by law and use available legal remedies to protect the client's rights.

Issues Requiring Attention

  • Legal grounds for the investigative action
  • Relevant procedural documents
  • Recording significant objections
  • Identifying seized materials
  • Preserving lawful business records
  • Assessing available complaint procedures

Lawful instructions from authorized officials must be respected during investigative actions. Disputed decisions and procedural violations should be challenged through legally available remedies, not by obstructing lawful investigative measures.

Economic Criminal Law Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I contact an economic crimes lawyer?

Seek legal advice as early as possible when summoned for questioning, facing a search or document seizure, receiving allegations of misappropriation or becoming involved in a potential criminal investigation.

Can a breach of contract amount to criminal fraud?

A contractual breach does not automatically establish fraud. Criminal liability requires proof of the relevant statutory elements and examination of the circumstances surrounding the transaction.

Can a director be held responsible for accounting department actions?

Criminal responsibility must be assessed individually. Relevant factors include the person's conduct, authority, mental state and the other legal elements of the alleged offense.

Can a financial expert report be challenged?

Expert findings are evaluated together with other evidence. Where justified, questions may be raised concerning the completeness of the examination, source records, methodology and procedural compliance.

What should I do if investigators search my office?

Comply with lawful instructions, identify the procedural grounds for the action and seek the assistance of an advocate where possible. Procedural objections should be recorded through legally available mechanisms.

Can a company be recognized as a victim?

This depends on the circumstances and the requirements of Kazakhstan's criminal procedure legislation. An advocate can assist with relevant applications and protection of the company's property interests.

Is an audit mandatory before reporting embezzlement?

An audit may be helpful, but it is not a universal mandatory prerequisite for every criminal complaint. The need for financial examination depends on the specific circumstances.

Can legal assistance be provided outside Almaty or Astana?

Representation in a particular region can be discussed after reviewing the location of proceedings, procedural stage, scope of work and practical arrangements.
