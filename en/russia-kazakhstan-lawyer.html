FAQ

Questions Russian Businesses Ask About Kazakhstan

Can a Russian company hire a lawyer in Kazakhstan?

Yes. If the matter involves Kazakhstan law, a Kazakhstan counterparty, court proceedings, assets, procurement or business operations, the work can often begin with a remote review of the relevant documents.

What if a Kazakhstan company does not pay for supplied goods?

The contract, performance documents, correspondence, pre-trial requirements, applicable law and dispute resolution provisions should first be reviewed. The appropriate recovery route can then be determined.

Can a Russian company participate in Kazakhstan public procurement?

Eligibility should be assessed for the particular procurement. Applicable legislation, procurement documentation, national treatment rules, exclusions, supplier status and the origin of goods, works or services may all be relevant.

Can an LLP be established in Kazakhstan with a Russian founder?

Kazakhstan law provides for registration of legal entities with foreign participation. Documentation and corporate structure requirements depend on the participants and the planned business activities.

Can a Russian court judgment be enforced in Kazakhstan?

Depending on the category of case and the applicable international framework, recognition and authorization for enforcement of a foreign judgment in Kazakhstan may be required.

Do we need to travel to Kazakhstan?

An initial assessment can usually begin remotely by sending the relevant documents electronically. The need for personal attendance depends on the specific procedure and circumstances.
