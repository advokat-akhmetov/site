Legal Practice in Almaty Region

Almaty Region has a distinctive economic and geographical position. It is closely connected with Almaty, Kazakhstan's largest commercial centre, borders China and Kyrgyzstan, and lies on major international transport routes.

As a result, legal practice in the region covers investment projects, international trade, manufacturing, construction, logistics, agriculture, real estate and tourism.

Khorgos – Eastern Gate Special Economic Zone has particular importance. Its infrastructure includes a dry port, logistics facilities and an industrial zone. For companies working with China, contracts, logistics, technical documentation, customs matters and payment arrangements should be coordinated before goods or equipment are actually shipped.
