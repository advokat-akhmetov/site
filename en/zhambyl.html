Legal Practice in Zhambyl Region

Zhambyl Region combines chemical and mining industries, agribusiness, construction materials, energy, transport, construction and investment projects.

Phosphate mining and processing, mineral fertilizers and other chemical production form an important part of the region's industrial profile. Projects in these sectors may create complex legal matters involving industrial equipment, contracting, construction, safety, environmental requirements, supplies and allocation of responsibility among project participants.

Taraz is the administrative, business and industrial centre of the region. Karatau and Zhanatas are closely associated with the mining and chemical sectors. Shu is important for transport, agriculture, industry and business activity.
