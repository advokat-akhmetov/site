EURASIAN ECONOMIC UNION

A common economic framework — different national jurisdictions

The EAEU consists of Kazakhstan, Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan and Armenia. Economic integration facilitates many cross-border activities, but membership in the Union does not make the national laws of all five countries identical.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the EAEU and Kazakhstan

Which countries are members of the EAEU?

The Eurasian Economic Union consists of Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Russia.

Can a Kazakhstan lawyer assist a Russian company?

Yes, where the matter is connected with Kazakhstan, such as a Kazakhstan counterparty, contract, debt, government authority, court proceeding or another legal relationship requiring Kazakhstan law.

Does the EAEU mean that all five countries have the same laws?

No. The Union provides common regulation in certain areas, while the member states retain their own national legislation and legal systems.

Can a foreign company recover debt from a Kazakhstan company?

The available recovery procedure depends on the contract, applicable law, jurisdiction, evidence of performance and the circumstances of the particular matter.

Can a foreign client work with a Kazakhstan lawyer remotely?

Initial analysis, document exchange and preparation of a legal position may generally be handled remotely. Further representation depends on the nature of the case.
