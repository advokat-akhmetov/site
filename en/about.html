Principles of Legal Work

Documents Before Conclusions A legal position is developed after reviewing the documents, factual circumstances and available evidence.
Risk Assessment Clients should understand not only the available legal options, but also the legal and commercial consequences of each course of action.
No Unfounded Promises The outcome of a legal matter depends on its circumstances, evidence, applicable law and decisions of the competent authorities and courts.
Confidentiality Information obtained in the course of providing legal assistance is protected in accordance with applicable law and professional confidentiality requirements.
Procedural Strategy In litigation, the applicable law is only one part of the case. Timely submission of evidence, motions and appeals may also be important.
Integrated Approach A single business dispute may involve civil, criminal, tax, administrative or customs law at the same time.
