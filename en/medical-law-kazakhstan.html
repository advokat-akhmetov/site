Medical Law

Healthcare law extends far beyond the doctor-patient relationship

Medical law covers a broad range of legal relationships: from protecting a patient in a medical dispute to establishing a clinic, regulatory matters involving medical devices, pharmaceutical products, equipment imports, public procurement and healthcare investment projects.

A single healthcare technology may simultaneously involve healthcare regulation, administrative law, civil law, contracts, customs, corporate law and procurement rules.

Legal work therefore should not begin only after a dispute has already arisen. In commercial healthcare projects, it is important to establish the legal structure in advance: identify the participants, review documentation, allocate responsibilities, determine applicable regulatory requirements and identify legal risks before market entry.

Risk Areas

Common legal risks in healthcare projects and disputes

Incomplete Medical Records

A dispute becomes harder to assess when the chronology of medical care and the decisions taken cannot be reliably established.

Formalistic Informed Consent

A signature alone does not necessarily answer what information was actually provided to the patient.

Poor Contract Structure

The functions and responsibilities of the clinic, intermediary, manufacturer, importer or distributor may not be clearly allocated.

Import Before Regulatory Review

Commercial commitments may be made before the lawful pathway for placing the product on the market is determined.

Unverified Foreign Manufacturer

Corporate documents, signatory authority, warranties, service obligations and liability are reviewed only after a problem arises.

Procurement Disputes

Problems may arise from technical specifications, admission decisions, procurement results, supply or contract performance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Medical Law in Kazakhstan

When may a patient need a medical law attorney?
Legal assistance may be required in disputes concerning the quality of medical care, treatment outcomes, access to medical records, informed consent, confidentiality, expert examination, compensation for harm or litigation.
Can a lawyer assist with medical device registration matters?
Yes. Legal support may include analysis of the regulatory and contractual structure of the project, manufacturer documentation and the legal relationships between participants in the regulatory process. Expert assessment and state registration are performed by the competent organizations and authorities.
Do you assist importers of medical equipment?
Yes. Depending on the project, legal work may include foreign manufacturer agreements, counterparty due diligence, regulatory matters, imports and customs, distribution, procurement and commercial disputes.
Can a healthcare provider receive comprehensive legal support?
Yes. Legal support may include business structuring, licensing matters, contracts, internal documentation, patient and counterparty relations, personal data, inspections, claims and litigation.
Do you assist Chinese medical device manufacturers entering Kazakhstan?
Yes. Legal support may cover the market-entry model, relations between the manufacturer and local importer or distributor, regulatory matters, international trade, customs, procurement, investment projects and contractual disputes.
Can a lawyer guarantee state registration of a medical device or medicinal product?
No. Decisions are made by the competent organizations and public authorities under the applicable regulatory procedures. The attorney's role is to provide legal analysis, support the relevant legal relationships and documentation, identify risks, protect the client's interests and use available appeal mechanisms where legally justified.
