LEGAL APPROACH

Registration is only one part of entering the Kazakhstan medical device market

Obtaining a registration document should not be treated as the entire market-entry project. Before commercial supplies begin, the manufacturer should determine the regulatory pathway applicable to the specific medical device, the roles of the participants, the import structure and the distribution model.

For an international manufacturer, the regulatory and commercial structures should be considered together. A poorly designed relationship with a local importer, representative or distributor may affect the manufacturer's long-term control over its Kazakhstan business.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about medical devices in Kazakhstan

Do medical devices need to be registered before sale in Kazakhstan?
The first step is to determine the legal status, intended purpose and applicable regulatory framework for the specific product. Where state registration is required, the relevant registration and examination procedures must be considered before commercial market entry.
Can a foreign manufacturer register medical devices for the Kazakhstan market?
Foreign-manufactured medical devices may enter the Kazakhstan market subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements. Before the process begins, the roles of the manufacturer, applicant or authorised participant, importer and distributor should be clearly structured.
Does a lawyer perform the regulatory examination?
No. Regulatory examination and state registration are carried out under the applicable procedures by the competent organisations and authorities. Legal counsel addresses legal structure, contracts, authorisations, documentation, risk allocation and protection of the client's interests.
Can medical devices be imported before registration is completed?
There is no universal answer for every product or transaction. The legal basis for import depends on the regulatory status of the product, the purpose of import and the applicable legal procedure. Specific situations may be subject to separate authorisation requirements.
Why should a foreign manufacturer review the distribution agreement separately?
The distribution agreement may determine territory, exclusivity, sales targets, payment, warranties, servicing, intellectual property arrangements and what happens when cooperation ends. These issues can directly affect the manufacturer's long-term access to the Kazakhstan market.
Can the entire Kazakhstan market-entry project be legally supported?
Depending on the project, legal support may cover partner due diligence, contractual structuring, medical device regulatory matters, import, distribution, public procurement, investment, localisation and dispute resolution.
