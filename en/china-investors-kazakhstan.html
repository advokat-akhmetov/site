CHINESE BUSINESS IN KAZAKHSTAN

The legal structure should be reviewed before capital is committed

For a foreign investor, registering a company is only one part of entering the Kazakhstan market. The project structure, ownership and control, site, contracts, regulatory procedures and protection of invested capital should be considered in advance.

A manufacturing or investment project may involve corporate law, land rights, construction, customs regulation, equipment imports, tax matters, Special Economic Zones, public procurement, commercial contracts and potential litigation or enforcement.

Practical approach: the earlier a project is legally reviewed, the greater the opportunity to identify material risks before contracts are signed, advance payments are transferred, equipment is purchased or construction begins.

INVESTMENT INCENTIVES

Do not build financial projections around incentives before eligibility is checked

Kazakhstan provides different investment mechanisms and special legal regimes. However, the availability of a particular preference may depend on the legal structure of the project, the applicant, the type of activity, the nature and amount of investment and compliance with applicable requirements.

Before an incentive is included in the financial model, the investor should determine which legal regime may apply and whether the proposed project meets its requirements.

DUE DILIGENCE · LEGAL OPINION

What should be checked before money is transferred to Kazakhstan?

Legal due diligence becomes particularly important where a project is proposed through a local partner, a land plot, an existing company or an operating manufacturing facility.

Who receives the money?

The contracting party, corporate authority and the legal relationship between the payment recipient and the proposed transaction should be reviewed.

Who owns the assets?

Land, buildings, equipment, company interests and other key project assets should be checked separately.

What rights does the investor obtain?

Ownership of an interest in a company does not by itself answer questions about actual control, governance or protection of the investment.

Which approvals are required?

The applicable procedures depend on the activity, land, construction, equipment and the characteristics of the project.

What is the partner promising?

Representations concerning land, SEZ status, incentives, government contracts or approvals should be compared with the actual documentation.

How can the investor exit?

The project structure should address what happens if obligations are breached, a shareholder dispute arises or financing is discontinued.

Legal Opinion: a separate written legal opinion may be prepared for an investment, manufacturing or construction project, identifying the proposed structure, material legal risks and possible risk-mitigation measures.

FAQ

Questions Chinese investors ask about doing business in Kazakhstan

Can a Chinese company establish a business in Kazakhstan?

The appropriate structure depends on the proposed activity, participants, regulatory requirements and the planned business model. The legal structure should be reviewed before the company and investment structure are finalized.

Can a Chinese investor establish a factory in a Kazakhstan SEZ?

Potential eligibility depends on the specific Special Economic Zone, the proposed activity, the structure of the project and compliance with the applicable requirements. The project should therefore be reviewed before the site is selected.

Does a foreign investor automatically receive investment incentives?

No. The availability of a particular preference depends on the legal regime, the applicant, the activity, the nature and amount of investment and compliance with applicable requirements.

How can a Kazakhstan business partner be checked before funds are transferred?

The review may include the company, corporate authority, transaction structure, proposed assets, land or property documentation and whether representations made by the partner are supported by the underlying documents.

Can a Chinese manufacturer participate in Kazakhstan procurement?

Participation depends on the particular procurement procedure, electronic platform, supplier requirements, product requirements and supporting documentation. Each procurement should therefore be reviewed individually.

What if a Kazakhstan partner refuses to return an advance or other funds?

The contract, payments, correspondence, actual performance, jurisdiction, evidence and information about the debtor should be reviewed. A legal strategy may then include negotiations, a formal claim, court proceedings and enforcement.
