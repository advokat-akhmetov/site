Legal Practice in Akmola Region

Akmola Region has several distinct economic centres. Kokshetau is the administrative and business centre, Kosshy is a rapidly developing city near Astana, Stepnogorsk has a strong industrial profile, while Burabay District combines tourism, construction, agriculture and investment projects.

A significant part of the regional economy is connected with agriculture, land use, agricultural processing, industrial production, mining, construction and infrastructure development.

As a result, legal practice in the region includes not only criminal and civil cases, but also land disputes, agribusiness, investment projects, construction, public procurement, contractual relations and legal protection of businesses.
