PHARMACEUTICAL LAW

Registration is only one part of pharmaceutical market entry

Bringing a medicine to the Kazakhstan market involves more than a registration procedure. A manufacturer should identify the applicable regulatory pathway, structure its relationship with the applicant or representative, determine the import model, establish contractual relations with distributors and plan the subsequent route to market.

Where a pharmaceutical product is intended for long-term supply to Kazakhstan, the legal structure should take the entire project lifecycle into account — from registration and the first shipment to procurement, replacement of a distributor, investment and possible localization of manufacturing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about medicine registration in Kazakhstan

Does a medicine need to be registered before it can be sold in Kazakhstan?
The applicable pathway depends on the legal and regulatory status of the specific product, the purpose of importation and the applicable regulatory framework. Before commercial market entry, the appropriate regulatory and registration route should be identified.
Can a Chinese or other foreign pharmaceutical manufacturer enter the Kazakhstan market?
Yes, subject to the applicable requirements. It is advisable to determine not only the registration pathway but also the legal relationship with the local representative, importer and distributor before market entry.
Does the lawyer personally perform the state registration procedure?
No. State, expert and other specialized procedures are carried out by competent authorities and organizations in accordance with the applicable legislation. The lawyer handles the legal aspects of the project, including contractual structure, authority, risk analysis and protection of the client's interests.
Can a medicine be imported and sold immediately after registration?
Registration status is only one element of the applicable legal framework. A particular shipment and subsequent commercialization may require analysis of import requirements, contractual arrangements, participants in the supply chain and other aspects of the project.
Why should a foreign manufacturer carefully structure its relationship with a distributor?
The distribution agreement may determine territory, exclusivity, purchase commitments, payments, commercial obligations and termination rights. An unsuitable structure may make it difficult to replace the partner or preserve the manufacturer's long-term position in the market.
Can legal support cover the entire project from registration to manufacturing in Kazakhstan?
Depending on the project, legal support may cover corporate structuring, contracts, registration matters, imports, distribution, procurement, investment, land, construction, localization of production and related disputes.
