For a foreign creditor, winning the case is only part of the process

The claim, evidence, debtor, jurisdiction and prospects of actual enforcement should be considered before court proceedings begin.

A cross-border dispute between a Chinese company and a Kazakhstan counterparty may involve contracts, specifications, invoices, bank transfers, shipping documents, acceptance certificates, WeChat or WhatsApp correspondence, emails and Kazakhstan corporate records.

Important: obtaining a court judgment and actually recovering the money are two different stages. Enforcement should therefore be considered from the beginning of the debt recovery strategy.

Contracts, specifications, supplementary agreements, purchase orders and other documents defining the parties' obligations.

Payments

WeChat & Correspondence

Acknowledgement of Debt

ASSET RISK · INTERIM MEASURES

What if the debtor may transfer or hide assets?

If failure to take measures could make future enforcement more difficult, the legal basis for requesting interim measures should be assessed.

Interim measures are not automatic. The court considers the circumstances of the case, the claims and whether the requested measure is appropriate and proportionate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions from Chinese companies

Can a Chinese company recover a debt from a company in Kazakhstan?

Where there is a valid legal basis, yes. Before proceedings begin, the contract, applicable law, jurisdiction, evidence, amount claimed and prospects of subsequent enforcement should be reviewed.

What if there is no complete written contract?

All available evidence should be reviewed, including payments, invoices, delivery documents, correspondence, acknowledgements of debt and evidence of actual performance.

Can measures be taken against the debtor's assets before judgment?

Where the legal requirements are met, an application for interim measures may be considered. The court decides whether the requested measure is justified and proportionate to the claims.

Will a Chinese company receive the money immediately after judgment?

Not necessarily. If the debtor does not comply voluntarily, further work through enforcement proceedings may be required.

Can a Chinese manufacturer participate in procurement in Kazakhstan?

Participation depends on the particular procurement procedure, supplier requirements, procurement documentation, electronic platform and applicable rules.

Can a Chinese factory be located in a Kazakhstan Special Economic Zone?

The specific project and SEZ should be assessed individually, including the type of activity, participant requirements, land, infrastructure, construction and other project conditions.
