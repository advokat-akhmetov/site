Can legal fees be recovered from the losing party in Kazakhstan?

Under Kazakhstan's civil procedural rules, expenses incurred for the assistance of a representative may be recoverable where the applicable legal requirements are met. Article 113 of the Civil Procedure Code addresses these expenses.

In property-related claims, the amount recoverable for representative's assistance is subject to a statutory limit of 10% of the satisfied portion of the claim. Different limitations apply to non-property claims.

Illustrative example: If a property claim for KZT 10,000,000 is fully satisfied, a 10% ceiling would equal KZT 1,000,000. This does not mean the court will automatically award that amount. Actual documented expenses and the applicable legal requirements must be considered.

Court filing fees, expert fees, translation costs and other expenses are not automatically included in an attorney's professional fees.

Can legal fees depend on the outcome of a case?

Kazakhstan law allows certain outcome-related fee arrangements subject to applicable legal restrictions. Such arrangements must be assessed in light of the specific matter.

Outcome-based fee arrangements should not be assumed to be permissible in criminal cases or administrative offence proceedings.

Any such terms require individual assessment and written agreement. No fee arrangement guarantees a particular court decision or legal outcome.
