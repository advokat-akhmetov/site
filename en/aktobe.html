Legal Practice in Aktobe Region

Aktobe Region has a strongly industrial economy. Oil and gas production, chromite mining, ferroalloy production, chemical industries, engineering, construction and transport logistics are important parts of the regional economy.

Aktobe is the administrative, business and industrial centre of the region. Khromtau is closely associated with mining and chromium resources, while Mugalzhar District is important for oil, gas and industrial projects.

This economic structure creates legal issues involving industrial incidents, criminal proceedings, investment, contracting, construction, equipment supplies, public procurement and commercial disputes.
