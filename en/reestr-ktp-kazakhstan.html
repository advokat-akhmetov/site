NEW FRAMEWORK FROM 2026

What changed for Kazakhstan manufacturers

The framework for confirming Kazakhstan manufacturer status changed from 2026. The Kazakhstan Manufacturers Register became the central mechanism for recording manufacturers and the goods they actually produce.

CT-KZ and the Industrial Certificate are no longer the main 2026 model

Manufacturers now need to focus on the applicable Manufacturers Register requirements, digital verification, actual production activities and compliance with the prescribed manufacturing and technological operations.

WHY THE REGISTER MATTERS

Registration can affect a manufacturer's access to the market

Inclusion in the Register may be relevant to participation in regulated procurement, access to certain state support measures and confirmation of actual domestic manufacturing status.

Public Procurement

Manufacturer status may be relevant where procurement legislation provides special conditions for domestic producers.

Manufacturer Status

The process looks beyond company registration and considers evidence of actual manufacturing activity.

State Support

Inclusion in the Register is connected with Kazakhstan's industrial policy and relevant support mechanisms.

COMMON BUSINESS PROBLEMS

Why a manufacturer may be refused registration

In practice, the problem may not be a single missing document. Refusals can arise when information submitted by the company does not correspond with its actual manufacturing activity or data available through state information systems.

01
Negative digital verification State information systems may not confirm sufficient indicators of the declared manufacturing activity.
02
Manufacturing not confirmed Equipment, premises, personnel and actual processes may not support the manufacturing model declared by the company.
03
Technological operations The manufacturer may fail to confirm performance of mandatory manufacturing or technological operations.
04
Product data discrepancies Product names, technical characteristics or classification codes may be inconsistent.
05
Production capacity Declared production volumes may not correspond to available equipment, personnel or actual capacity.
06
Video evidence and inspection Submitted materials may be insufficient to demonstrate that the required production operations are actually performed.
DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

Turnover and E-Invoices

Imports and Exports

Production Resources

REGISTRATION PROCESS

How registration in the Manufacturers Register works

The exact process depends on the product and manufacturing model. Before filing an application, it is important to review not only the documents but also whether actual production meets the applicable requirements.

01 Preparation Company, products, production facilities, equipment and data.
02 Application Submission of the required information through the state system.
03 Verification Digital assessment of indicators of manufacturing activity.
04 Assessment Review of production and required technological operations.
05 Decision Inclusion in the Register or another reasoned outcome.

REFUSAL

What to do if registration is refused

The first step should not be to resubmit the same application. The specific grounds for refusal should be identified first: documents, digital verification, manufacturing operations, technological requirements or product identification.

A refusal should be analysed by its specific legal and factual basis

The appropriate strategy may differ. In one case the company may need to correct an actual deficiency; in another it may need additional evidence; in a third case it may be appropriate to assess available appeal mechanisms.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Manufacturers Register

What is the Kazakhstan Manufacturers Register?

It is a state information system containing information about Kazakhstan manufacturers and the goods they produce.

Why can registration be refused?

The exact reason should be determined from the decision. Issues may arise from digital verification, actual production, technological operations, production capacity, product information or submitted evidence.

Can a company reapply after a refusal?

Further action depends on the original grounds for refusal. Before reapplying, it is generally important to identify and address the deficiencies or inconsistencies that led to the adverse decision.

How is the Manufacturers Register related to the EAEU TN VED?

Product classification information, including the EAEU TN VED commodity code, is relevant to product identification. Correct classification can therefore be important when describing the goods actually manufactured by the company.

Can a company be checked after it is included in the Register?

Yes. The current framework provides for subsequent monitoring of manufacturers' compliance with applicable requirements.

Can removal from the Register or another adverse decision be challenged?

The type of decision, applicable appeal procedure and relevant deadlines should first be identified. Depending on the circumstances, administrative or judicial protection may be available.
