COMMODITY CLASSIFICATION

A TN VED code is a legally significant classification of goods

Goods should not be classified solely by their commercial or everyday name. Classification may depend on their objective characteristics, including composition, materials, construction, functions, intended use, degree of processing and completeness.

₸

Customs payments

Commodity classification is connected with the applicable customs duty rate and may therefore directly affect the amount of customs payments due on importation.

01

Restrictions and permits

Classification may affect certification, licensing, permit requirements and other restrictions applicable to the movement of particular goods.

02

Post-release customs control

Classification may be reviewed not only during customs clearance but also within customs control procedures after the goods have been released.

LEGAL METHODOLOGY

How an EAEU TN VED code is determined

Proper classification does not begin by searching the internet for a similar product. It begins with identifying the actual characteristics of the goods and applying the legal rules governing the EAEU Commodity Nomenclature.

1 Identify the goods Establish the actual technical and functional characteristics.
2 Heading analysis Review potentially applicable headings and subheadings.
3 Legal notes Examine relevant section, chapter and heading notes.
4 GRI Apply the General Rules for the Interpretation of the TN VED.
5 Classification practice Review rulings and official materials concerning comparable goods.
Kazakhstan customs legislation. Classification under the EAEU TN VED is based on the applicable rules of interpretation, legal notes and the objective characteristics of the goods. Depending on the case, official classification materials and prior rulings may also be relevant to the legal analysis.

A photograph or the product name appearing on an invoice may therefore be insufficient. For machinery, for example, relevant characteristics may include the operating principle, principal function, capacity, component structure and the manufacturer's technical documentation.

An HS or commodity code stated by a foreign supplier in an invoice, packing list or export declaration may be useful evidence, but it does not replace an independent classification under the EAEU TN VED.

IMPORTER RISKS

What can happen if the commodity code is incorrect

Incorrect classification can have consequences beyond a technical amendment to a customs declaration.

Additional customs payments A different classification may result in another customs duty rate and raise the issue of additional customs payments.
Permits and compliance documents A different code may trigger certification, licensing or other regulatory requirements that were not considered before shipment.
Customs clearance delays A dispute over the characteristics of the goods, requests for additional documents or expert examination may complicate customs clearance.
Post-release review Classification issues may arise after the goods have already been released, sold or placed into operation.
BEFORE IMPORTATION

Advance ruling on commodity classification

Where classification is complex or the financial consequences of an incorrect code are significant, Kazakhstan customs law provides a mechanism for obtaining an advance classification ruling before customs declaration.

10

Decision period

The applicable legislation provides a procedure and time limit for considering an application for an advance classification ruling. The period may be affected where additional information or examination is required.

3

Validity

An advance classification ruling under Kazakhstan customs legislation is generally valid for three years from the date on which it is adopted.

10

Commodity code

The ruling identifies the relevant commodity classification under the EAEU TN VED together with information material to the classification.

Important: an advance ruling concerns goods with specific identifying characteristics. A ruling obtained for one model, article or modification should not automatically be treated as covering technically different goods.

The application should therefore be supported by documentation allowing the customs authority to identify the goods and their classification characteristics. Current filing procedures and available government service channels should be checked against the official rules applicable on the filing date.

EVIDENCE

Documents used to analyse commodity classification

The more technically complex the product, the more important the supporting technical evidence becomes. A defensible legal position should be based on verified characteristics rather than the product name alone.

  • full commercial description of the goods;
  • brand, model, article number and modification;
  • technical passport and manufacturer datasheet;
  • description of intended use and operating principle;
  • composition and materials;
  • photographs, diagrams and drawings;
  • manufacturer catalogue or operating manual;
  • international trade contract and specification;
  • commercial invoice and packing list;
  • export declaration from the country of dispatch, if available;
  • previous rulings concerning comparable goods, if relevant;
  • expert opinions where they are material to the dispute.
Supporting evidence may include technical documents, accompanying commercial documents, expert materials and other information relevant to the objective characteristics of the goods. However, the mere appearance of a commodity code in a commercial document does not automatically make that code binding on Kazakhstan customs authorities.
CUSTOMS DISPUTES

If Kazakhstan customs changes the declared TN VED code

In a classification dispute, it is generally not enough to state that the supplier used the same code or that previous shipments were cleared under it. The classification reasoning itself should be examined from both a legal and technical perspective.

1 Obtain the decision Identify the grounds relied upon by the customs authority.
2 Identify disputed characteristics Determine which properties of the goods are material to the dispute.
3 Review the GRI Compare the authority's reasoning with the classification rules.
4 Build the evidence Technical documentation, classification practice and expert evidence.
5 Challenge the decision Determine the appropriate administrative review procedure.
Administrative challenge: when challenging an administrative act or action of a Kazakhstan public authority, the applicable requirements of the Administrative Procedural and Process-Related Code, special customs legislation and the appeal instructions contained in the particular decision should be considered.
MACHINERY & PRODUCTION LINES

Complex and multi-component equipment requires particular care

Classification of production lines, industrial installations and equipment shipped in parts should not necessarily be determined solely by the individual descriptions appearing on commercial invoices.

Principal function

For multifunctional machinery, it may be necessary to establish the function and technical purpose relevant under the applicable classification rules.

Goods shipped in parts

Special classification considerations may apply to unassembled or disassembled goods and equipment supplied in multiple consignments.

Technical evidence

Engineering drawings, specifications, installation descriptions, component lists and manufacturer catalogues may become central evidence in a classification analysis.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about HS and TN VED codes in Kazakhstan

Who determines the TN VED code when goods are imported into Kazakhstan?

A commodity classification code is declared during customs declaration. Kazakhstan customs authorities may verify the classification and, where provided by law, adopt a classification decision.

Can an HS code be determined from a product photograph alone?

A photograph may assist an initial review, but a legally supported classification often requires information concerning composition, materials, function, intended use, construction and other technical characteristics.

Can I obtain an official classification before importing the goods?

Yes. Kazakhstan customs legislation provides a mechanism for an advance classification ruling before customs declaration. The application must contain sufficient information and documentation to identify and classify the goods.

How long is an advance classification ruling valid?

Under Kazakhstan customs legislation, an advance classification ruling is generally valid for three years from the date on which it is adopted.

Is the HS code provided by a Chinese manufacturer binding on Kazakhstan customs?

No. The supplier's code may be useful information, but classification in Kazakhstan is determined under the EAEU TN VED rules and by reference to the actual characteristics of the goods.

What should I do if customs changes the declared code?

The classification decision and its reasoning should be reviewed, the disputed characteristics identified, the applicable GRI and legal notes checked, supporting technical evidence assembled and the appropriate administrative challenge procedure determined.
